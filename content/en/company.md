Ditchbridge was born from a field observation: after fifteen years spent organising and securing major sporting events, its founder measured how much operational teams lacked a tool genuinely designed for their profession — a tool capable of supporting them in the moment, not simply documenting what had already happened.

## A company built by and for operations

Ditchbridge was founded by Simon Patard, who spent ten years in the event operations of Paris Saint-Germain and three years at the organising committee of the Paris 2024 Olympic and Paralympic Games, alongside Charles Sabourdin, a senior software architect specialising in security and the production deployment of critical systems. This dual culture — field event expertise and demanding software engineering — is at the foundation of Ditchbridge's positioning: building a tool from the real needs of operational teams, rather than adapting a generic tool to the world of events.

## A strong academic foundation

Ditchbridge was built in close collaboration with the research community, particularly with the Industrial Engineering centre of IMT Mines Albi, which originated the technology that inspired the platform. The company is supported by the IMT Mines Albi incubator and the Lanceur d'Étoiles programme, and maintains exchanges with several support structures in Occitanie. This foundation gives Ditchbridge continuous access to the latest advances in artificial intelligence applied to decision-making and the modelling of complex systems.

## A clear position in a fast-growing market

The market for operational event management platforms is growing steadily, driven by the digitalisation of sites, the rise of AI applied to operations, and increasingly stringent safety requirements. This market remains fragmented: existing players each focus on a particular segment — incident management, access control, mapping, timing — without any having established itself across the full operational lifecycle of an event, from preparation to post-event review. That is precisely the space Ditchbridge intends to occupy, as a unified operational command platform, built for events and large-scale gatherings.

## An ambition that goes beyond events

While events are Ditchbridge's first deployment territory, the logic of real-time management of complex, multi-stakeholder systems that the platform carries is intended to extend, over time, to other sectors facing the same challenges of critical coordination — energy, water, and transport. Ditchbridge is developing along a progressive trajectory, resolutely oriented towards operational excellence and European technological sovereignty.
