title: The Platform
---

Managing a live event means constantly navigating the unexpected: a flow that accelerates, a gate that saturates, a team that must be redeployed within minutes, in an environment where all stakeholders must stay synchronised. Ditchbridge was designed precisely for this reality: to give organisers and operators a single operational command platform, capable of understanding a situation, anticipating its evolution, and orchestrating the response in real time.

## A unified view of the deployment

Ditchbridge aggregates data from the various systems present on an event site — flows and attendance, field teams, access, alerts, communications — and delivers them as a digital twin of the site, readable at a glance. The operations centre has a common situational picture shared with teams deployed in the field, where today's tools remain mostly siloed by function or zone.

## Anticipate rather than react

The distinctive feature of Ditchbridge lies in its decision engine, designed to detect early warning signals of deterioration before they become an incident: congestion at a gate, access saturation, a delay propagating through the site. Rather than simply surfacing incidents after they occur, the platform anticipates and proposes an explainable, reasoned response plan built on experience from previously encountered cases. The decision-maker can then validate or adjust the operational response.

## From decision to action, without gaps

Once a decision is validated, Ditchbridge orchestrates its execution: every responder receives their task in real time on their field application, accepts it, executes it, and confirms completion. Operations command tracks the response in real time with full traceability of all actions taken — a continuous chain from data, to decision, to action, with no re-entry or handoff gaps between teams.

## A system that learns from every event

Every event managed through Ditchbridge enriches a shared operational repository: decisions made, resolution times, and effective response patterns are capitalised to refine future recommendations. At the close of each event, a debrief report is automatically generated, consolidating the complete chronology of facts, decisions, and outcomes — a valuable tool for both operational post-mortems and demonstrating the performance of the safety deployment.

## Technology rooted in fifteen years of research

Ditchbridge builds on research conducted over more than a decade at the Industrial Engineering centre of IMT Mines Albi on the modelling of complex collaborative systems and decision support in crisis situations. This technology, originally designed for crisis management, has been specialised and adapted by Ditchbridge to the specific demands of event management and large-scale gatherings: high human density, multiple stakeholders, and the time pressure of live operations. These challenges are found across many other sectors, opening a broader future for Ditchbridge beyond event management alone.

## Built for live operations, not just preparation

Many tools on the market are primarily designed for upstream planning (mapping, logistical organisation) or for targeted functions (access control, timing, accreditation). Ditchbridge takes a different position: as the transverse orchestration layer that connects these systems and supports teams precisely where the stakes are highest — on the day, in real conditions, when every minute counts.

Its digital twin function also allows Ditchbridge to simulate procedures and exceptional situations before operations begin, to stress-test safety plans, train teams to collaborate on realistic scenarios, and arbitrate resource allocation before a deployment's weaknesses emerge under real conditions. Preparation and live operations share the same model of the site, guaranteeing complete continuity between training and real execution.
